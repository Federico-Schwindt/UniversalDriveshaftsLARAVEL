Rule three

Buzz or shake?

DriveshaftBuzzFast, felt in the floor, console and shifter. Often blurs the mirror. The shaft spins 3–4× faster than the wheels.
TiresShakeSlow, 55–65 mph, felt in the seat and the steering wheel.
Clunk on gear changes + highway buzz = a u-joint with play. Quiet at low speed, grows with speed.
